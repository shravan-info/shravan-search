Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What this website does

Shravan Search is a static job-discovery directory. It helps visitors find links to external employer career pages and job platforms. It does not provide recruitment, accept job applications, or create user accounts.

Information stored in your browser

The website may use your browser's local storage only to remember your light or dark theme preference. Search text and filter choices are used in the current page to filter the public directory and are not submitted to a Shravan Search server by this website.

Third-party websites

When you open an external career website, that website may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms. Review those policies before sharing personal information or applying.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website changes. Check this page for the latest version.
